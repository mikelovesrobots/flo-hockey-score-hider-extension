# Privacy Policy for FloHockey Score Hider

Last updated: [Current Date]

## Overview

FloHockey Score Hider is a Chrome extension designed to hide score displays on FloHockey.tv to prevent spoilers. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data Collection

FloHockey Score Hider:

- Does not collect any personal information
- Does not track your browsing history
- Does not store any data
- Does not communicate with any external servers

## Permissions Used

The extension uses the following permissions:

- `activeTab`: Required to modify the content of FloHockey.tv pages to hide the score display
- Host permissions for `*://*.flohockey.tv/*` and `*://*.hockeytech.com/*`: Required to access and modify content on these domains to hide the score

## How the Extension Works

The extension works entirely locally in your browser:

1. It detects when you're on a FloHockey.tv page
2. It modifies the page content to hide the score display
3. No data is sent to any external servers
4. No data is stored locally

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page.
